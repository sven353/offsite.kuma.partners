The problem

The Offsite Paradox: High Energy, Zero Velocity on Monday

01 · The Travel Trap

Great dining and scenic views, zero intellectual architecture.

Outcome: an expensive corporate vacation that leaves dysfunction untouched.
02 · Artificial Harmony

C-suite peers avoid hard debates in plenary rooms; real conflict festers in hallways.

Outcome: polite meetings; unaddressed friction drives attrition.
03 · Theoretical Fluff

Facilitated by generic mindset coaches detached from P&L and runway.

Outcome: post-it notes with zero operational accountability.
04 · The Monday Cliff

High bonding evaporates in 48 hours because no decision rights were codified.

Outcome: teams return to identical bottlenecks by Monday morning.
